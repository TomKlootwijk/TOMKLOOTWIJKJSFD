A new operational profile, preserving v0.7
Authorial corpus: Tom Klootwijk. Identifier: NL200678942. Date as supplied: 10-07-1990. Release: TOM v0.7.1. This
operational extension was specified on 19 September 2026 following the author's instruction to add executable
behavior. The identifying details record authorship; they are not physical constants, encryption keys or simulation
parameters.
v0.7.1 retains the whole v0.7 definition and assigns one explicit continuation profile to previously open behavior.
The chosen Thue-Morse jitter and exact golden-ratio recurrence are new model assignments. They are not claimed
to follow from v0.7, to have been present in the original corpus, or to predict physical events.
TOM := InverseOccam[Arch[C[J[T]]; E[C[J[T]]; InvLog[phi]]]] qualified_by forward_T_precedence
The abbreviations are c := C[J[T]] and e := E[c; InvLog[phi]]. Semicolons distinguish ordered roles. Expansion
changes spelling, not the definition. SDF continues to mean the native admission qualification: it does not assert a
metric or a normalized signed-distance function.
What this release makes definite
A committed state supplies exact coefficients a and b. At the next admitted simulation tick, the profile computes a
deterministic jitter sign and derives a new state. It then constructs the exact echo, retains both ordered roles,
records the zero scalar reading without replacing the definition, and commits the complete step only if time,
evidence and capacity checks pass.
In plain language: the earlier rulebook said how to preserve the past and wait for a valid next step. This version
also specifies one rule that calculates that next step. It is a repeatable mathematical process with an inspectable
history; it is not a model of every phenomenon that might later be described using TOM.
Governing source and status
The unchanged parent is TOM_V0_7_Formalization.pdf (18 pages), SHA-256
97582a16bd54d690b27105f9b09dc48d2b5716bbae2fff2a5e74190278640fbc. Its pages 3-9 define the binding and
structural contracts; page 14 identifies open assignments. The original PDF and package are retained. This
document is a specification, not a claim that acceptance tests or a performance benchmark have passed. Execution
evidence belongs in a separately dated results report.

The structural commitments remain in force
Every admitted defining role, compound and admissibility rule remains SDF-qualified. T, J, lowercase phi, InvLog, C,
E, Arch and InverseOccam retain their roles. The profile imposes no intrinsic coordinate grid, vector space,
operator graph, quaternion arena or global Psi enclosure. Computer records and their operand addresses are a
disclosed external representation.
Commitment
Requirement
TC1 / no anticipation
Use only supplied initial evidence available at or before the committed simulation time. Deriving a
simulated future is not possessing an observation of a physical future.
TC2 / completed prefix
Never alter a committed record or its order. A correction is a later record or a separately declared
new experiment.
TC3 / no time reversal
An inverse-log role or a negative coefficient does not reverse the temporal qualification.
TC4 / guarded
self-reference
Own-name declaration is permitted. The result of continuation becomes available only after the
next step is derived and admitted.
TC5 / elapsed time
Each admitted step advances by its declared positive delta. Nesting or a zero readout does not
remove that elapsed duration.
Only licensed equivalences
InverseOccam[InverseOccam[x]] =def InverseOccam[x]
The other licensed representational changes are c/e/TOM abbreviation expansion and A =def T only when
explicitly declared. A single InverseOccam wrapper is not identity. Ordered Arch and E operands cannot be
swapped, flattened or reassociated by appealing to a symmetric numeric reading. There is no implicit InvLog
involution.
Equality of selected readings does not imply equality of definitions. Normalization retains the original source and
original terms. A finite representation may share equivalent storage, but it must not erase the defining distinction
or its provenance.
There is no fixed intrinsic nesting depth. The executable has declared storage and address bounds; failing to fit is a
realization failure, not a new source law. Klein and Matryoshka retain their named roles. This release assigns
neither a Klein topology nor a nested physical construction to those names.

Exact numbers, jitter and the next state
phi = (1 + sqrt(5))/2; phi^2 = phi + 1; kappa = -ln(phi) < 0
C_n = a_n + b_n*phi, with a_n,b_n in the integers
The default seed is (a_0,b_0)=(0,0), with n=0 and time=0. A caller may explicitly supply any representable signed
integer pair and nonnegative step index. Such a seed defines the start of that experiment; it does not assert that
an earlier history has been supplied. An arbitrary seed is not interchangeable with resuming an existing saved
experiment.
J_n = (-1)^popcount(n)
J_0 = +1; J_(2n) = J_n; J_(2n+1) = -J_n
This is the signed Thue-Morse sequence, selected as a deterministic jitter law. It is not random noise, a measured
frequency, thermodynamic entropy or a security primitive.
C_(n+1) = phi*C_n + J_n
(a_(n+1), b_(n+1)) = (b_n + J_n, a_n + b_n)
The coefficient update follows by substituting phi^2=phi+1. Computational states use signed integer limbs and
exact symbols only: no floating values, approximate square roots, logarithms or divisions enter the transition. The
irrationality of phi makes (a,b) a unique representation of a+b*phi.

Role
Selected v0.7.1 meaning
T
The current committed index n, simulation time t, positive delta and supplied horizon H.
J[T]
Compute the exact sign J_n from the current n.

Use the committed coefficient pair and the evaluated jitter operand to derive (b+J_n, a+b).
InvLog[phi]
The exact named constant kappa=-ln(phi). Its representation is symbolic, not a rounded decimal.
E[C; InvLog[phi]]
Construct the exact real expression kappa*(a_next+b_next*phi) from its actual evaluated

Arch[C; E]
Retain the ordered direct/echo pair and calculate the selected external scalar reading as exactly

InverseOccam[...]
Preserve the defining expression, the original supplied source and the complete committed

forward T qualification
Admit and publish a step only after the transition and all temporal/capacity obligations succeed.
E_(n+1) = kappa*(a_(n+1) + b_(n+1)*phi)
arch(x,y) = |x| + |y| - |x-y|
arch(C_(n+1), E_(n+1)) = 0
The zero result is exact: kappa is negative and the arguments have opposite signs or are zero. The echo stores the
derived integer coefficients together with the exact kappa tag. There is no loss of precision merely because a
transcendental constant is kept as a defined symbol. This profile does not evaluate arbitrary expressions involving
transcendental constants.
Execution must dispatch through the actual admitted term records and operand references. It must reject a
changed program that is no longer the full source expression under licensed equivalences. Running an unrelated
recurrence beside a decorative copy of the expression does not satisfy this contract.
SELF and Later_T
The continuation driver uses SELF to refer to the actual committed coefficient state. Later_T is realized by the next
admitted transition. Its result is the derived coefficient pair and preserved echo/Arch record; the caller never
supplies a next-result coefficient pair as a substitute for computation. This profile admits only the complete TOM
root, not arbitrary alternate programs or unrestricted recursion.

A tick is an atomic, bounded transition
A request supplies an initial or resumed state (n,t,a,b), delta>0, a simulation horizon H, the latest available
initial-evidence time, and a nonnegative requested step count. The same delta applies throughout that run. The
supplied evidence time must not exceed t. The initial t must not exceed H. The units of these tags are explicit
experiment parameters; no physical clock is authenticated.
Phase
Exact behavior
Validate
Reject malformed encodings, unsupported program shape, negative zero, zero delta or an initial
time beyond the horizon. Reject future initial evidence.
Check next tick
First require room for a complete record; then require n+1 and t+delta to fit their 64-bit fields;
then require t+delta <= H. A failed check leaves the current head unchanged.
Derive
Evaluate J_n and the full program; compute exact candidate coefficients, echo and ordered Arch
result in disposable scratch.
Check storage
Require all candidate coefficients and the entire next history record to fit the declared capacities.
Never wrap, truncate, erase, silently resize or substitute a floating result.
Commit
Publish one complete record and update (n,t,a,b) together. Then proceed to the next requested
step.
Atomicity is per step. If a request commits three steps and the fourth fails, the first three remain committed. The
failing step publishes no partial record and makes no change to the committed head. A request for more records
than S can retain commits up to S successful steps and then reports history capacity, unless an earlier obligation
stops it.

Initial validation can combine INVALID and FUTURE_EVIDENCE. An admitted run stops at its first failed check, in
the order above; program evaluation follows retained term order. A zero status means the requested steps
completed. Counts describe the successful prefix. Benchmark repeats restart the same seed; they are not
additional evolution. A new run may explicitly change delta or horizon without rewriting its prior head.

Exact storage, observation and resume
The executable codec is part of this external profile. Words are little-endian uint32. A coefficient has a sign (0
positive/zero, 1 negative) and L little-endian base-2^32 magnitude limbs. Zero must have sign 0. Its magnitude is at
most 2^(32L)-1. Index, time, delta, horizon and evidence tags are unsigned 64-bit values.
The source is retained as exact UTF-8 bytes, with zero padding only to the next word boundary. Original
eight-word native term records and the original root are retained. Normalization licenses are the v0.7 licenses, not
a new rewrite system. The Python source compiler checks source-to-term binding; a C++ host that retains arbitrary
source text does not thereby parse or authenticate that text.

N is the retained term count and S the next-run record capacity. Host size arithmetic must be checked before
allocation. The file envelope has 16 words: magic, ABI version, case count, L, S, N, original root, flags, case words,
result words, record words, UTF-8 byte count, archived prior-file words, then three reserved zeros. Input magic is
0x314f4d54 and result magic is 0x31524d54. ABI version is 1. The only program flag is bit 0 for explicit A=T.
After the envelope come the padded source bytes, original terms, the complete archived prior output (if any), and
current cases. Fresh input files have no archive. A resumed output nests the entire previous output byte for byte,
including its previous archives. Complete prior content is retained, not replaced by an unchecked hash or a count.
Each committed record stores input n, before/after time, jitter sign, before/after coefficient signs and magnitudes,
exact echo coefficients and the kappa tag, scalar zero readout and qualification flags. These are simulation
observations of the defined process. They do not acquire a physical interpretation merely by being called
observations.
Before resume, the host re-evaluates every archived seed using its retained program and compares every saved
result word. It also checks each segment starts from the preceding terminal coefficient pair, n and t, case by case.
Limb capacity may grow without changing the integer value; it may not shrink. The new input must match the
latest saved head. A zero-commit failure leaves that head equal to the original seed.
Buffers are reserved before execution. Output paths may not alias inputs or prior/reference artifacts. Checked
temporary writes and atomic file replacement preserve an existing destination on publication failure. Multiple files
are not one atomic transaction. There is no power-loss durability, cryptographic authorship or adversarial
tamper-authentication claim; consistently rewriting an unauthenticated experiment cannot in general be detected
from its own contents.

Consequences, acceptance and the boundary of
this release
Consequences of the selected laws
Determinism: fixed seed, program, parameters and capacities give the same committed prefix and stop condition.
Exact closure: multiplication by phi maps the integer pair (a,b) to (b,a+b), so adding J keeps the state in Z[phi].
Strict progress: each successful transition adds one to n and a positive delta to time. Preservation: each commit
appends a complete record; rejection leaves the prior head. Distinct histories remain distinct even when their final
coefficient pairs or scalar zero readings agree.
These are consequences of the specification, not a formal verification certificate for a particular binary. Exactness
holds within the announced coefficient/address capacities; exhaustion is reported rather than approximated. The
default recurrence may revisit a coefficient pair while its index, time and retained history continue forward.
Required executable evidence
Validation must compare all result words with an independently implemented integer reference, including the first
twelve steps; test changed full-expression bindings, licensed aliases and idempotence; exercise large positive and
negative seeds; and test coefficient, clock, horizon and history boundaries. Resume must agree with uninterrupted
evolution where parameters agree. Corrupt saved states, traces and inconsistent source/program bindings must
be rejected within the stated parser/trust scope. Output collision and failed-publication checks must preserve
existing files.
A performance report must name the workload, number of evolved steps, exact capacities, memory use, timing
scope and verification method. Filling GPU memory is not proof of peak arithmetic utilization. A measured result
must not be advertised as exhaustive exploration of every TOM expression or as a forecast of real events.
Assigned here; still open beyond this profile
Assigned here: deterministic jitter, an exact coefficient recurrence, a symbolic inverse-log echo, step/horizon
actualization, a simulation observation record and a recoverable finite archive codec. The inverse-log and entropic
names do not become measured physical mechanisms. No thermodynamic entropy, physical measurement
protocol, general intrinsic pinion dynamics, Klein topology, autonomous discovery mechanism or arbitrary-SDF
execution semantics are supplied.
A later profile may define different dynamics while preserving the v0.7 commitments. Such an extension must
state its added law, its interpretation and its own validation. v0.7.1 is fully specified for this particular operational
profile; it does not claim that every possible application or physical meaning of the corpus has been implemented.
