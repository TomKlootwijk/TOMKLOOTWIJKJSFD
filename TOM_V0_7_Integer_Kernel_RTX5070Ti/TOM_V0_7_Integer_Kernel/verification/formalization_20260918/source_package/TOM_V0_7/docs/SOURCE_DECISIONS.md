## Evidence labels

**Source mandate:** an explicit author instruction quoted or paraphrased with a
page location. **Exported proposal:** the accompanying response's suggestion;
not automatically an accepted axiom or a proved claim. **V0.7 law:** a new formal
clause introduced and identified for this release. **Diagnostic result:** a
consequence proved under the explicitly selected scalar reading. **External
realization:** a future numerical, digital or physical interpretation, never
silently equated with the intrinsic definition.
