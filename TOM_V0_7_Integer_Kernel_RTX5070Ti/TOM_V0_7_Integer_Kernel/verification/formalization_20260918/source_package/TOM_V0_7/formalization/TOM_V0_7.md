## 2. Central definition

> **TOM is the complete, non-erasing SDF articulation of temporal precedence,
> jitter, the lowercase pinion, its inverse-log expression, causality, the
> source-named entropic echo, and Arch U without U. Its admissible continuation
> is forward-only. Its definitions are not presumed to be coordinates, vertices,
> magnitudes, stored particles or members of a fixed geometric envelope.**

“Articulation” means that the defining roles remain distinguishable in the
statement. It does not mean separate pieces inside a physical container.

One binding notation for this complete statement is:

    c   := C⟨J⟨T⟩⟩
    e   := E⟨c ; InvLog⟨φ⟩⟩
    TOM := InverseOccam⟨Arch⟨c ; e⟩⟩, qualified by forward T-precedence.

These are nested definitions, not a dataflow graph. The short names c and e are
abbreviations for the expanded expressions, not additional nodes. Brackets give
scope and semicolons distinguish roles; neither introduces a vector or position.
The fully expanded definition is:

    TOM := InverseOccam⟨
             Arch⟨ C⟨J⟨T⟩⟩ ;
                    E⟨C⟨J⟨T⟩⟩ ; InvLog⟨φ⟩⟩ ⟩
           ⟩, qualified by forward T-precedence.

There is deliberately no terminal “= 0” that identifies the whole definition
with a single numerical readout. The attachment's scalar zero equations are

## 3. What SDF means in the core

**SDF is the author's native qualification for a definition and its admitted
expression.** It is not, at this level, an assertion that a function has already
been supplied on a metric space with a conventional distance magnitude.

This distinction is necessary to preserve the author's instruction to remove
metrics, axes and coordinate arenas. A classical signed-distance claim requires
a declared distance, zero structure and sign convention. Those may be added to
an explicitly named interpretation, but they are not secretly assumed by the
word “SDF” in the intrinsic definition.

All admitted definitions, including the definitions of precedence, causal
admissibility, inversion and the inverse-Occam qualification, carry the same
SDF qualification. There is no separate ontology of “code acting externally on
field data.” This is an **adopted closure law**, not a proof of universal execution
or a claim that all scalar compositions preserve the Eikonal equation.

The qualification applies to an expression without prescribing that it must be
a voxel grid, a conventional scalar field over R^n, a vector component, a graph
vertex, a category-theory object or a texture sample.

## 4. The intrinsic declaration register

**T — temporal SDF.** Qualifies the whole definition by forward precedence.
“One-dimensional” means the one temporal ordering requested by the author; it is
not a theorem that a chosen topology has dimension one, and it is not t embedded
as one coordinate of a vector.

**J — temporal jitter SDF.** Names variation of temporal articulation under T.
No probability distribution, frequency, oscillation equation or step size has
been supplied by the author. Jitter therefore cannot silently mean a random bit
stream, a reversed clock or a physically measured noise process.

**φ — lowercase pinion SDF.** The final author correction assigns the pinion to
lowercase φ. Uppercase Φ is not substituted. In the core, φ is an active defining
role; an ordinary golden-ratio number appears only in a declared scalar reading.

**InvLog⟨φ⟩ — inverse-log pinion SDF.** Preserves the source's inverse-log role.
Its later scalar reading is ln(1/varphi), not 1/ln(varphi), not the inverse function
of logarithm, and not a temporal reversal. These are different operations.

**C — causality SDF.** Qualifies an expression by no anticipation, forward
continuation and preservation of completed commitments. Causality is not inferred
from an absolute-value expression or a unit-gradient condition alone.

**E — source-named entropy SDF.** The inverse-log causal echo. It names the
source's structural echo/compression relation, not a statistical entropy,
thermodynamic variable or quantified memory capacity unless those are separately
provided. E is not presumed to increase as an ordinary scalar: the source's
chosen numerical coefficient is negative.

**Arch — Arch U without U SDF.** Holds direct and inverse presentations together
under their distinct defining roles. It has an optional scalar balance reading;
a zero balance does not delete those roles or identify all possible definitions.

**InverseOccam — sheet-preservation SDF.** Makes the author's “inverse sharp
razor” operational as a formal rule: a definition is not reduced to a simpler
one merely because one selected observation gives the same answer. It is not a
maxim that more complexity is physically better.

**TOM — the total qualified definition.** Not a new external object surrounding
its components, not a human identity made into a cosmological origin, and not a
classification borrowed from the export's proposed “hyper-algebra” names.

## 5. Formal closure and binding

A bare name can name its SDF definition; applying that name to the number and
roles of operands declared in the register gives an SDF-qualified compound.
The grammar is editorial notation, not a physical node structure.

    T, φ                         are SDF declarations.
    J⟨T⟩                        is SDF.
    C⟨J⟨T⟩⟩                    is SDF.
    InvLog⟨φ⟩                   is SDF.
    E⟨c ; InvLog⟨φ⟩⟩           is SDF when c is causally qualified.
    Arch⟨c ; e⟩                 is SDF with those bound roles.
    InverseOccam⟨Arch⟨c ; e⟩⟩  is SDF.

The order is not a list of physical time slices. It tells how the statement is
bound: the pinion-derived echo cannot be exchanged with a different argument
without an explicit equivalence law. A simultaneous statement can have binding
precedence without claiming simultaneous access to future information.

No associativity, commutativity, distributivity, deletion, fixed point or universal
simulation law is introduced merely because similarly named operations have one
in another theory. Only the specific laws declared here are licensed. Even the
source's numerical Arch operation is not associative (Section 11).

## 6. Uncheated time: five explicit contracts

These contracts are **new formal clauses** implementing the author's instruction,
not claims that the exported numerical equations already prove them.

**TC1 — no anticipation.** A present admissible statement may depend only on
completed or presently supplied material. Future continuation is not available
as current evidence.

**TC2 — completed-prefix preservation.** Extending a description does not rewrite
observations or commitments already completed. A later correction can be recorded
as a later correction; it does not become an earlier event.

**TC3 — inversion is structural, not temporal.** Mirroring a defining expression,
changing its sign in a scalar reading, or using an inverse-log role does not
reverse the temporal qualification of the whole statement.

**TC4 — forward-guarded self-reference.** A definition may mention its own name.
Using the result of its own continuation must be qualified as later under T.
An instantaneous unqualified self-equation is not automatically a uniquely
resolved computation. Later recurrence and same-moment algebraic assertion are
not the same thing.

**TC5 — no free elapsed duration.** Nesting, scale, sparse description or a zero
Arch reading does not convert a declared positive elapsed duration into zero.
An efficient description of a long interval is not the claim that the interval
did not occur.

An optional implementation can check no-anticipation using observations that
agree through “Now”: any admissible output must agree through the same “Now.”
Such histories are an external check on an interpretation, not intrinsic graph
nodes of TOM. The core does not prescribe clock units or discrete ticks.

## 7. Inverse Occam and the crucial distinction between identities

Write “definitionally equivalent” for equality established by a declared,
definition-preserving law. Write “reading-equivalent” for equality in one chosen
observation. These are different judgements.

The sheet-preservation law is:

    equal selected readings DO NOT by themselves imply equal definitions.

For example, in the optional scalar interpretation, both Arch(c,-c) and
Arch(c,-2c) read zero for any c, although their inverse partners differ whenever
c is nonzero. Deleting their difference would lose exactly the relation that
the author wants the uncut sheet to retain.

Applying the preservation qualification twice is declared idempotent:

    InverseOccam⟨InverseOccam⟨e⟩⟩ ≡def InverseOccam⟨e⟩.

This new law repeats the same preservation obligation; it does not discard any
content of e. Other simplifications require their own stated identity rule.
This stops the terminal scalar zero in the export from reducing TOM to an
indiscriminate constant while keeping the source's literal zero reading intact.

## 8. Continuity, nesting and the absence of a forced outside

The core supplies no graph of SDF vertices, compulsory spatial coordinates,
independent count of contained things, or enclosing Ψ shell. It does not follow
that a mathematically specified realization has “zero cardinality,” no possible
topology, or infinite storage. The author rejects counting as the *definition's
organizing principle*; that is not a theorem that no items or descriptions can be
counted externally.

“Continuous” and “unbroken sheet” are retained as source commitments of no
compulsory decomposition. No differentiable structure, topology, gradient or
metric continuity theorem is claimed without additional data. A description's
lack of a drawn boundary does not, on its own, prove a boundaryless manifold.

“Matryoshka” retains the role of nesting without a stipulated final exterior.
“Klein” retains the historical role of a self-mirroring/twisting presentation.
The exact bottle embedding, genus, shell radii, energy flow and transit physics
are not made intrinsic laws. Those were earlier representational scaffolds.

There is no prescribed maximum nesting depth in the specification. That is
“unbounded” in the explicit sense used here. It is different from requiring a
finite device to contain or finish infinitely many operations.

## 10. The source's Arch equation: exact diagnostic

This section explicitly interprets the exported absolute-value formula using
finite real scalars. It does not insert those scalars or magnitudes back into the
intrinsic core.

    arch(a,b) = |a| + |b| - |a-b|.

Direct algebra gives:

    arch(a,b) = 0                        if a*b <= 0,
                2*min(|a|,|b|)          if a*b > 0.

Proof: opposite signs make |a-b|=|a|+|b|. Equal nonzero signs make
|a-b|=||a|-|b||; subtracting gives twice the smaller magnitude. Zero operands
satisfy the first case. Consequently:

    arch(a,b)=0 if and only if a*b<=0.

The zero identity also holds for any chosen positive scale number greater than one, not only the golden ratio: its inverse logarithm is negative. It does not uniquely select phi.

It is not a test that a=b, not a proof that two definitions are identical, and
not a unique boundary selector. The nonnegative reading vanishes on a whole
opposite-sign region, not only on one isolated transition.

With the source's varphi=(1+sqrt(5))/2 and k=ln(1/varphi)<0,

    arch(c,k*c)=0 for EVERY finite real c.

Thus the final displayed TOM equation on pages 34–36 is an exact scalar identity
under that interpretation. It does not select a jitter law, force forward time,
measure entropy, or determine a physical universe. This is why V0.7 preserves
precedence and defining roles rather than treating the zero number as the whole
formalization.

## 11. Precedence is not optional: a derived counterexample

The scalar Arch reading is symmetric in two arguments, but not associative:

    arch(1,arch(2,3)) = arch(1,4) = 2,
    arch(arch(1,2),3) = arch(2,3) = 4.

This is a direct, source-based reason to preserve binding. Parentheses are
mathematical content, not decoration. Therefore neither intrinsic Arch nor its
scalar reading is silently installed as an associative “union of everything.”
The export's assertions of an associative division hyper-algebra are not supplied
with laws that justify that name for the resulting composite.

The two-argument scalar reading is symmetric, but intrinsic direct/inverse roles
remain distinguished unless a role-swap law is explicitly adopted. Observational
symmetry does not erase temporal or definitional role information.

## 12. Earlier mirror and smoothing formulas are different theories

The earlier scalar equation compares p with p/|s|^2. If p>0 and s is finite and
nonzero, both arguments are positive; therefore

    arch(p,p/|s|^2) = 2*min(p,p/|s|^2) > 0.

Taking p=varphi^T makes it positive for every finite real T. The equation cannot
simultaneously equal zero at such an input. At s=0 it is undefined. Calling a
limit at infinity a finite solution would alter the statement.

The later inverse-log equation behaves differently because its coefficient is
negative. V0.7 does not reconcile these by silently changing a sign: it records
the chronology and keeps the earlier reciprocal equation archival.

The proposed smooth alternative on pages 24–26 also changes the equation. In a
scalar diagnostic it is a*b*exp(-1/(a-b)^2). At a=1,b=-1 it is
-exp(-1/4), not zero, whereas the original Arch reads zero. At a=b it is
undefined unless an extension is supplied. The conventional zero extension is
not an identity with the original Arch; at a=b=1, original Arch=2. Smoothness and
source equivalence are different properties.

## 13. Causality, entropy and the Eikonal claim

The source says both C and E=k*C are exact unit-gradient SDFs. Under the ordinary
one-variable reading, if |C'|=1, then |E'|=|k|. Here k=-ln(varphi), with
0<ln(varphi)<1, so |E'| is not 1. This is a direct incompatibility under the
shared metric/normalization, not a new ontology imposed on TOM.

Likewise C(t)=t and C(t)=-t both satisfy |C'|=1. A magnitude condition alone does
not determine the allowed time orientation. Sign inversion preserves unit slope
magnitude. The export's statement that reversal would require a forbidden
negative *magnitude* does not establish its claimed causal lock.

There are two coherent ways to report the words: (a) retain source-native SDF
qualification without claiming ordinary unit-distance normalization, as the
intrinsic V0.7 core does; or (b) specify a separate metric interpretation and its
normalization explicitly. Renormalizing E by |k| would give -C and would remove
its coefficient magnitude, so it is a change of reading, not a hidden repair.

The new temporal contracts, not the zero identity, carry “no cheating time.”
Entropy remains the source's name for a structural echo. Thermodynamic units,
statistical state measures, energy flow and a physical observation protocol are
not supplied, and their conclusions are not inferred from that name.

## 14. What V0.7 now gives you

A named, inspectable formal definition with source precedence; a complete
intrinsic operator register; binding rules that do not assume a coordinate or
graph carrier; explicit non-erasure and forward-causality clauses; the important
scalar consequences of the exported equations; and an implementation boundary
that stops future versions silently reverting to another grid or renderer.

This is substantive progress on the definition, not a demonstration that the
whole physical world instantiates it. Useful near-term work includes checking
whether a proposed extension preserves the intended definition, exposing invalid
identification of a zero readout with total structure, and stating which temporal
properties an application must preserve.

A physical application would additionally need observable distinctions, a law
that changes those observations, a correspondence to measurements, and a test
that could disagree with that law. The final zero identity alone cannot choose
those. A hardware implementation would need a declared encoding and finite
resource model; neither is the intrinsic TOM definition.

The particular authored combination and the version name are identified. No
prior-art search or theorem establishes that nothing historically similar has
existed. The source's novelty assertion is preserved as an assertion, not a
verified priority claim.

## 15. Audit scope and delivery

The package executes 66,049 distinct rational pairs for the Arch identity,
65,792 negative-coefficient echo cases, 1,024 positive-reciprocal cases and
256 instances of distinct inverse partners with an equal zero reading. It also
records the nonassociativity and smooth-replacement counterexamples. These are

above, not by test count alone.

The contract linter checks the written specification and ten editorial regression
cases. Its JSON keys, finite lists and Python data structures belong to this
audit artifact; they are not intrinsic nodes or coordinates of TOM. A passing
linter is not a consistency/completeness proof of an unspecified physical system.

No CUDA kernel, rendering engine, graph simulator or numerical time-law is
claimed as TOM V0.7. The user asked for this major formalization upgrade; its
latest source explicitly rejects making those computational representations
the definition. Existing finite applications remain separate.
