A presently admissible expression may depend only on completed or presently supplied material.

TC2 / Completed-prefix preservation

resolved behavior.

disappear. Compressing a description of an interval is not proving that its elapsed time was zero.

14 How previous applications relate to TOM
INTRINSIC DEFINITION VERSUS OPTIONAL REALIZATION
Earlier releases explored packed-bit logic, graph routes, sparse crowds, CUDA textures and event
histories. Those can remain useful finite application models. The new source, however, explicitly
rejects treating such representations as the definition of its continuous, non-node-based articulation.
[S37, pp. 14, 26, 28, 31]
A realization must declare what it preserves
A future digital, numerical or physical interpretation must identify its correspondence to the native
SDF roles. It must state which defining distinctions remain visible, how causal precedence is
maintained, what data it can observe and which equivalences it actually preserves.
A finite graph representation would be an external projection with declared loss or interpretation,
not evidence that the intrinsic SDFs were graph nodes all along. A bit-level encoding similarly
needs its own storage and execution contracts. A finite audit program is not TOM merely because
its output discusses TOM.
What this gives you now
You have an authored definition that can be read without choosing a spatial embedding or replacing
the source with an existing mathematical category. It includes explicit temporal requirements, a
defensible interpretation of the inverse-Occam phrase, a source-decision register and reproducible
checks of the exported scalar formulas.
This is useful for reviewing future versions: a claimed implementation can be checked against
these commitments instead of accepted on a visual analogy or a zero numerical residual. The
formalization also identifies which operations and empirical statements need more than a name.
Physical claims remain a different task
To claim a law of nature, one would additionally need a stated observable distinction, a law that
predicts it, a mapping to measurements and a test that could disagree. Neither a sign identity nor
the removal of a coordinate system supplies those on its own.
No new empirical result hidden in the terminology
The release does not identify a person with a cosmic continuum, certify an unprecedented
discovery, infer infinite storage or claim faster hardware. The source’s own page-23
clarification places the identity back in authorship, where it is retained exactly.
