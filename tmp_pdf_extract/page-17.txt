15.1  Relational inheritance
Across these mappings, the recurring relation is finite source, paired transfer, changing field definition, 
limiting condition and LUT-assigned update. The ballistic passage changes the subject of the relation from 
morphology to projectile kinematics; it does not supply a distinct working trajectory model, material model 
or experimental calibration.
