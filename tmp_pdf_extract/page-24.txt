A2.1  Early four-component representation
P_Klein(u,v) = (
  (R + r cos u) cos v,
  (R + r cos u) sin v,
  r sin u cos(v/2),
  r sin u sin(v/2)
)
u,v in [0, 2π)
SDF_Klein(x) = ||x - P_Klein(u,v)||
This records the displayed host expression and parameterization before the author’s temporal redirection. 
The source’s printed distance expression does not assign a parameter-selection or minimization step. 
[S, pp. 3-4]
A2.2  Suspension tensor
W_Kuiper = _C ρ(θ) ·∮
           [∇f_A(x_θ) ⊗ ∇f_B(x_θ)] dθ
C is the source’s closed belt curve; ρ(θ) is its density factor;  is its outer-product notation. The next ⊗
source page displays an arrest relation using the time derivative of f_Klein multiplied by Tr(W_Kuiper), 
followed by “  0.” [S,⇒  pp. 4-5]
