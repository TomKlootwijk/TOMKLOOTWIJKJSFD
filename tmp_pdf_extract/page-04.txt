Core vocabulary
SOURCE  /  S, pp. 1-12, 15-17, 19-26, 30-39
The following terms retain their source-specific meanings. Short labels in the left column are edition 
notation for referring to the same objects throughout the specification.
Term Definition within the architecture
SDF operator A formal boundary or relationship definition. The early source evaluates scalar geometric fields; 
the later source uses intrinsic, coordinate-free operator relationships.
Klein / K The non-orientable host operator and inversion relation carried with every packed operator.
Double packing The simultaneous SDF and Klein definitions of an operator, evaluated according to the 
applicable precedence chain.
AB One binary word. Its source-defining states are 0: exterior, and 1: double-packed evaluation.
Psi / Ψ The source’s bifurcation designation for the binary shift and its lower-phi temporal scaling.
Lower-case phi / φ The approximately 0.618 relation assigned to compression, damping and convective turning.
Diabolo / hourglass The paired-domain transfer relation with a neck or choke point; later treated without a required 
coordinate geometry.
W_Kuiper The suspension tensor weight linking A and B. This is distinct from the later timeline symbol W.
LUT The kinematic, log-encoded polar or spherical look-up table that names permitted state 
resolutions.
W and Operator T W names the late-source one-dimensional wave timeline. T is the phase-scrubbing operator 
acting on that timeline.
Atomos The architecture name and the node designation used when the source expands its scope to the 
total world population.
Kiki double-UU The named set/routing operator assigned non-intersection and self-closing loop behavior.
Hot node / hum blip A source-defined active signature in the double-sinusoidal 2 Hz evaluation layer.
Negative memory Deviation or vacancy relative to a saturated baseline, rather than an accumulated inventory of 
novel records.
JIT geometry A coordinate or geometry output evaluated for the current request and dropped after use.
