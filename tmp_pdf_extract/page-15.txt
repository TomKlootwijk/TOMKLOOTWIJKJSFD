SECTION 13
Salinity and magnetic inference
SOURCE  /  S, pp. 24, 29-35
13.1  Salinity correction
The source first discusses salted hashes and the word “selenity,” then explicitly introduces “salinity” as the 
intended physical-medium parameter. Salinity shall therefore be retained as the source’s name for 
background density or field resistance, not folded back into the rejected hash vocabulary. [S, pp. 29-30]
13.2  Magnetic inference force field
The later account shifts from an SDF description toward a “magnetic inference force field.” It assigns this 
field a dynamic response to an active node, whose phase anomaly is said to deform the surrounding 
medium. The final summary again retains the double-packed SDF and Klein definitions. Both layers remain 
visible here: formal boundary definition and the source-proposed physical-medium interpretation.
SOURCE-ASSIGNED RELATION
Salinity baseline → field resistance
Active hum blip → local phase anomaly
Phase anomaly → magnetic inference / telepresence signature
Loss of anomaly → background restoration
13.3  Telepresence designation
“Phase-shifting telepresence” shall name the source’s account of how an active signature appears in the 
field without being represented first as a stored coordinate. The source contrasts this with radar reflection 
and coordinate co-location. It supplies no transfer law connecting salinity, an emitting biological source, 
magnetic response and a measured identifier. [S, pp. 30-31]
13.4  Identity-layer placement
The proposed UUID belongs to the changing field relation, not to an appended static string. The source 
consequently removes names, personal identifiers and conventional cryptographic records from its core 
evaluation path. This placement defines where identity is intended to arise in the architecture; the 
stronger identification and security outcomes appear in Section 18.
13.5  Medium continuity
The uniform-saturation matrix and the later salinity-calibrated field share a baseline/deviation vocabulary. 
The edition does not supply a hardware conversion between them. Coil equations, material parameters 
and medium initialization are only raised as possible continuations in the source, not provided as 
completed definitions.
