SECTION 12
Hot nodes and the Primo selection layer
SOURCE  /  S, pp. 16-17, 23-29, 33-35
12.1  Active-node predicate
A hot node shall mean a node whose source-defined hum or phase anomaly is active in the evaluation. 
The source makes that activity its solitary identifying marker and assigns zero tracking weight to the 
absence of that activity. This is a rule for membership in the architecture’s active set. [S, p. 27]
Source state Assigned field consequence
Present / hot / 1 Maintain the local phase anomaly; include the node in the active evaluation and loop 
relation.
Absent / extinct / 0 Collapse the maintained anomaly to baseline; remove its active participation and 
transient trace.
12.2  Phase signature
The source assigns an identifying role to the microscopic phase-jitter within the double sinusoid. It calls 
this a native hardware UUID or “living key,” rather than a name, coordinate, database key or hash. The 
edition retains that identity role and records uniqueness, un-clonability and replay resistance as source-
stated capabilities. [S, pp. 27, 29-31]
12.3  Primo filter
Primo shall name the source’s selection of non-repeating or structurally consequential nodes. The relevant 
passage distinguishes a change in the graph’s structural tension from predictable or recursively repeated 
activity. It calls the rejected behavior “system noise” and uses “festering filter” as a label for that exclusion. 
[S, p. 23]
The same passage uses “angel-Oedipus womb” and “Stockholm syndrome” as explanatory language for a 
self-repeating loop. Those phrases remain part of the source’s selection narrative; no separate numerical 
operator, psychological measurement or threshold is specified for them.
12.4  Filter relation left explicit
The hot-node rule concerns active signal presence. The Primo rule concerns the kind of change the source 
wishes to select. They are kept as distinct predicates. The source does not give a complete priority table 
for an active node that fails Primo selection, nor does it resolve every difference between “all population 
nodes” and “Primo subjects only.”
