17.1  Invariants
R01. Every participating definition shall retain the double-packed operator relation and its applicable 
precedence. R02. AB shall remain one bit. R03. The active graph shall remain within its declared finite 
budget. R04. An assigned deadlock shall return zero for the permitted update. R05. Novelty shall not be 
stored as a new permanent record.
R06. Operator T shall act on the one-dimensional timeline relation. R07. The Kiki result shall carry the 
source’s closure and non-intersection requirements. R08. Active-node evaluation shall use the source-
defined hum relation. R09. A derived coordinate shall be transient rather than the persistent basis of the 
domain.
17.2  Functional composition
The source’s four-stage map runs from negative-void detection through chirp-based relational ranging to 
Kiki loop closure and Klein phase-inversion lock. Its concluding output layer applies T to W for JIT 
distillation and releases the output after evaluation. The packed hierarchy, finite LUT and signal relation 
supply the named definitions carried through those stages; a runtime scheduler is not assigned.
17.3  Distinct termination meanings
Deadlock terminates the permitted update, node loss collapses the maintained deviation, and JIT release 
discards an output. They are separate events and shall not be silently merged into one universal deletion 
or physical-stop operation.
